IT Support Knowledge Base — Remote Access

VPN Access
Employees must use the CompanyVPN client to connect to internal systems from
outside the office. VPN credentials are separate from your email login and
must be requested from the IT Helpdesk. VPN sessions automatically time out
after 8 hours of inactivity.

Password Policy
Passwords must be at least 12 characters long and include one uppercase
letter, one number, and one special character. Passwords expire every 90
days. Employees cannot reuse their last 5 passwords.

Laptop Requests
New employees receive a company laptop within 3 business days of their
start date. Laptop requests are submitted through the IT Helpdesk portal,
not through email.

Help Desk Hours
The IT Helpdesk is available Monday to Friday, 8:00 AM to 6:00 PM. Outside
these hours, urgent issues can be reported through the on-call emergency
line, which is monitored 24/7.

Software Installation
Employees cannot install unapproved software on company laptops. All
software requests must go through the IT Helpdesk and be approved by a
manager before installation.
